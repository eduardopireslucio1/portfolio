EventosXP

A white-label platform for sports events. The organizer's brand up front, EventosXP running the operation.

Each organizer gets a registration site of its own, with its name, domain, colors and sponsors. Athletes find the race, register and pay by Pix or card in a single flow. Behind the start line, one panel handles participants, payments, categories, batches and check-in.
